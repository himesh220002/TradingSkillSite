Community Pulse

Ideal Communication Architecture for TradingXSkill

1. Structural Ecosystem

The community is divided into **Global Lounges** and **Batch-Locked War Rooms** to balance general networking with focused learning.

graph TD A[Student Entry] --> B{Role Check} B -->|Free/Public| C[Global Lounge] B -->|Enrolled| D[Batch War Rooms] D --> E[Specific Batch Room] E --> F[Q&A Thread] E --> G[Trade Logs] E --> H[Live Stream Chat] C --> I[General Discussion] C --> J[Success Stories]

2. Trainer-Student Feedback Loop

Ensuring trainers can broadcast high-signal information while students get their queries resolved without noise.

sequenceDiagram participant S as Student participant T as Trainer participant B as Bot / AI S->>B: Asks "What is RSI divergence?" B->>S: Suggests Lesson 4 Video Link S->>T: Still confused, tags Trainer T->>S: Records 30s Loom Video Response T->>S: Marks Query as "SOLVED" Note over S,T: Solution added to batch Knowledge Base

3. Trade Setup Verification Flow

A gamified way for students to share charts and get peer/trainer validation.

graph LR A[Share Setup] --> B[Upload Chart Image] B --> C[Add Strategy Tag: SMC/PriceAction] C --> D[Community Voting] D -->|Upvoted| E[Trending Setup] D -->|Trainer Verified| F[Model Trade Badge] F --> G[Leaderboard Points]

4. Core Pillars

Threaded Q&A

Instead of messy group chats, use threaded discussions linked to specific course modules for easier searching.

Analysis Showcase

A dedicated gallery for students to post their daily technical analysis for peer review.

Live Trade Floor

A real-time text-based "floor" for quick signals and market updates during live trading hours.

Institutional Broadcasts

Tutors can push "Urgent Market Shifts" or "Class Postponed" notifications directly into the community feed.
